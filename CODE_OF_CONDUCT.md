# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior includes:

- Treating people with respect and good faith.
- Giving constructive, technically grounded feedback.
- Respecting privacy, boundaries, and different backgrounds.
- Focusing discussions on the work rather than individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent.

Report problems privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
